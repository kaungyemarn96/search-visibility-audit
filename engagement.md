# Engagement record — bundle 02

Extends `core/engagement-record.md`. What this bundle specifically must capture, and when.

---

## Before touching anything

- [ ] **Full crawl export.** Every URL, with title, description, status and depth. This is the before-state and it is unrecoverable once fixes land.
- [ ] **Screenshots of search results** for the brand term and the two or three target terms. Cheap now, impossible later.
- [ ] **Answer-engine baseline.** Every standing prompt, every engine, answers recorded verbatim with citations. The single most persuasive artifact in the whole engagement, because the before-state is usually the client not being mentioned at all.
- [ ] **Both score sets.** Letter grades and the four dimensions, with the tool, date and settings recorded.
- [ ] **Technical baseline**, mobile and desktop separately, noting the tool and conditions.
- [ ] **Existing schema**, saved as found. Needed to prove what was already there when a later regression is blamed on this work.
- [ ] **Existing robots.txt and sitemaps**, saved verbatim before replacement.
- [ ] **Analytics baseline** for the metric set that will be reported against.

## During

- [ ] **Every value handed over**, and the date. Where the writer determines and someone else applies, the record of what was handed over is the only proof of delivery.
- [ ] **Every handover receipt.** Who confirmed, when.
- [ ] **Anything handed over and not applied**, with how long it has been outstanding. Not for blame. It is the evidence behind the next scope note, and it explains a flat report.
- [ ] **Validation result URLs** for every schema type.
- [ ] **Decisions the client overruled**, with their reason.
- [ ] **Hours against each deliverable**, feeding the next fixed-price quote.

## At close

- [ ] **Re-run both score sets**, identical settings.
- [ ] **Re-run every standing prompt**, verbatim, same conditions.
- [ ] **The delta, honestly**, including anything that moved the wrong way.
- [ ] **After-state crawl**, same tool and settings as the before.
- [ ] **What was handed over versus what went live.** The gap is the most useful thing this record produces.

## Permissions to ask for

Ask at close, while the client is pleased, and ask for the three separately:

**Name** — may the organisation be named. **Numbers** — may specific figures be published, or only ranges and directions. **Artifacts** — may a score dashboard, a before-and-after answer, or a sitemap be shown, and does anything need redacting.

A before-and-after answer-engine pair, permitted for publication, is worth more as evidence than any traffic figure. Ask for it specifically.
